## Uses that do not require separate permission

You may:

- truthfully state that software is based on, compatible with, or a fork of Tether;
- link to this repository and reproduce unmodified screenshots for commentary, education, or documentation;
- use the name in an unmodified redistribution that clearly identifies the upstream project;
- describe conformance to a published Selfsame Protocol level when the claim is accurate and links to evidence.

## Uses that require permission or a different name

You may not use the name or marks in a way that suggests an unofficial fork, hosted service, commercial product, organization, or modified distribution is maintained, certified, sponsored, or endorsed by the Tether project.

Materially modified distributions should choose a distinct primary name and say “based on Tether” rather than presenting themselves as the official Tether release. Do not reuse official artwork as the primary identity of a separate service.

Selfsame Protocol is an implementation-independent specification. Truthful compatibility and conformance statements are welcome, but they must not imply certification unless the project publishes a certification process.

Questions about uses not covered here may be raised in a repository discussion without disclosing confidential product plans.
